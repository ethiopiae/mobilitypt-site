It depends on your insurance. California law lets you start physical therapy without a doctor's referral, within limits. But many insurance plans have their own rules. HMO and medical group plans usually need a referral and an authorization from your doctor before your first visit. When you book, we check what your plan needs and let you know.

" - q: How many visits will I need? a: "

It's different for everyone. It depends on your injury or surgery, your goals and your insurance. After your evaluation, your therapist recommends a plan of care and talks it through with you. Your plan may change as you progress.

" - q: Can I choose one-on-one or small-group sessions? a: "

We offer both. Some patients do best with focused one-on-one time, and some enjoy the encouragement of a small group. Tell us what you prefer, and your therapist will help you decide what fits your care.

" - q: What if I'm running late? a: "

Please call the clinic you're visiting as soon as you can. We'll let you know your options for that day.

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. Your therapist will want to see how the sore area moves.

  • For a knee, hip or ankle: shorts or loose pants that roll up easily
  • For a shoulder, neck or back: a T-shirt or loose top
  • Supportive shoes, like sneakers

Why your full plan matters

{%- if site.draft %}

Draft copy for the clinic to review.

{% endif %}

Most of the progress in physical therapy comes from steady work over time. Each visit builds on the last one, and coming regularly helps you get the most out of your plan.

That's why we book your whole plan with you at your first visit. Your times are saved, and you can keep your momentum going. If something comes up, call us and we'll help you find a new time.
