How we care for you

Every plan is built around you: your injury, your goals and the life you want to get back to.

One-on-one sessions

Focused, personal time with your therapist, with hands-on guidance along the way.

Small-group sessions

Work alongside others on the same road. A little company brings encouragement and helps you keep going.

Exercise made for you

Your exercises are chosen for your body and your goals, and they change as you get stronger.

Education and prevention

{%- if site.draft %}

Draft copy for the clinic to review.

{% endif %}

Getting better is only part of the goal. We want you to understand your body well enough to stay active on your own.

  • We explain what's going on and why each exercise helps.
  • You leave with your own exercises and stretches to keep doing at home.
  • We share everyday habits that can help lower the chance of the next injury.

Every person is different. Always follow the advice of your own therapist and doctor.
