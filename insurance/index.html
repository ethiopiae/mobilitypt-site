HMO and medical group plans

These plans often need a referral and an authorization from your doctor before physical therapy starts. Ask your doctor's office to send the referral to the clinic you're visiting.

PPO plans

PPO plans usually don't need a referral. Your copay, coinsurance or deductible still applies. We'll check your benefits before your first visit.

Medicare Part B

You usually don't need a referral to start, but your doctor will need to sign off on your plan of care. Your Part B deductible and coinsurance apply.

Auto MedPay after a car accident

We work with auto MedPay from all carriers. Bring your claim number and adjuster's contact details. What to do after a car accident
