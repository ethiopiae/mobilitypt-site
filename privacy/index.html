Website privacy

This section covers this website only. Your care at our clinics is covered by the HIPAA notice above.

No ad trackers

This site does not use advertising trackers, tracking pixels or social media pixels. We don't sell or share your information with advertisers.

What our forms collect

Our website forms ask only for what we need to call you back, such as your name, phone number, preferred clinic and, for coverage checks, your insurance company or claim number. They never ask for medical details. We use them only to get back to you.

Please don't send health information through website forms or email. Regular email and web forms are not the right place for medical details. Call the clinic instead, and we'll talk it through.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies only when a feature of the site needs them to work. If we add analytics to learn which pages are helpful, it will be privacy-friendly and will not track you across sites.

Our clinic pages show a Google Maps map, and the site loads its font from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address and set its own cookies when those load. See Google's privacy policy.
