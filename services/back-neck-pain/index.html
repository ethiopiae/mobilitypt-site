Common back and neck problems

Back and neck pain are among the most common reasons people see a physical therapist. Problems physical therapists commonly treat include:

  • Low back pain, whether it came on suddenly or has built up over time
  • Sciatica-type symptoms, such as pain, tingling or numbness that travels into the leg
  • Neck stiffness and pain that makes it hard to turn your head
  • Posture-related pain from long hours at a desk, in a car or looking down at a phone
  • Disc-related pain, when your doctor has told you a disc may be involved
  • Mid-back tightness and pain between the shoulder blades

What your visits look like

Your first visit is an evaluation. Your therapist asks about your pain and daily life, checks how you move, and explains what they find in plain words. Together you set goals that matter to you.

After that, visits usually mix hands-on care, exercises to build strength and mobility, and practical tips for sitting, lifting and sleeping. You also get a home program, so you keep making progress between visits.

How long recovery takes

Everyone heals at a different pace. Your therapist will give you a plan after your evaluation and update it as you improve.

When to call a doctor or 911 instead

Call 911 or go to the emergency room if back or neck pain comes with chest pain, trouble breathing, loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness in the groin or inner thighs, or new weakness in your legs. Also get emergency care after a serious fall or crash.

Call your doctor soon if your pain comes with a fever, unexplained weight loss, pain that is worse at night and doesn't ease with rest, or numbness or weakness that is getting worse. This page is general information, not medical advice. Always follow your therapist or doctor's advice.

Insurance notes

We work with Medicare Part B, many PPO plans and several local medical groups. If your plan is an HMO or medical group, you'll likely need a referral from your doctor first. We'll check your coverage before your first visit. See the plans we work with or read whether you need a referral.
