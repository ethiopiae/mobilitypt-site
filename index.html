From injury to independence

We go beyond recovery. We help you build the knowledge, confidence and strength to stay healthy for the long run.

Back and neck pain

Spine care for the neck, mid-back and low back. Less pain now, and habits that protect your back for life.

Recovery after surgery

Safe, structured progressions after surgery to get your movement back and return to daily life with confidence.

Joint pain, sprains and strains

Individual programs to restore mobility, improve strength and get you back to doing what you love.

Car accident injuries

Back and neck injuries after a crash. We work with auto MedPay coverage.

Work injuries

Rehab for on-the-job injuries, with workers' compensation accepted.

Education and prevention

You leave with your own exercises, stretches and health strategies to prevent the next injury.
