What we help with

From a sore back to the months after surgery, we treat the problem and teach you how to keep it from coming back.

Back and neck pain

Spine care for the neck, mid-back and low back. Less pain now, and habits that protect your back for life.

Recovery after surgery

Safe, step-by-step progressions after surgery to get your movement back and return to daily life with confidence.

Joint pain, sprains and strains

Individual programs for knees, shoulders, hips, ankles and more, to restore mobility and strength.

Car accident injuries

Whiplash, back and neck injuries after a crash. We work with auto MedPay coverage.

Work injuries

Rehab for on-the-job injuries, with workers' compensation accepted.

Staying well after therapy

You leave with your own exercises, stretches and know-how to prevent the next injury.

One-on-one when you need it. A small group when it helps.

One-on-one sessions

Small-group sessions

Your first visit

Simple from the first call.

  1. 1

    Call or request online

    Pick the clinic closest to you. We'll find a time that works.

  2. 2

    We check your coverage

    We confirm your insurance and whether your plan needs a doctor's referral.

  3. 3

    Your evaluation

    Bring a photo ID, your insurance card and your new patient forms, plus your calendar to book follow-ups.

  4. 4

    A plan you can follow

    Treatment in the clinic and exercises for home, adjusted as you improve.

Save time at check-in. Fill out your new patient forms before you come.
