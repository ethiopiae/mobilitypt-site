What happens at your evaluation

1. We talk about your history

Your therapist asks what happened, what hurts, what makes it better or worse, and what you want to get back to doing. Tell us about past injuries, surgeries and any medicines you take.

2. We test how you move

We check your range of motion, strength, balance and posture, and how the problem area handles everyday movements. Tell us if anything feels like too much. We'll adjust.

3. We make a plan together

Your therapist explains what they found in plain words and sets goals with you. The plan covers how often to come in and what we'll work on.

4. You start your exercises

Most people leave with a few starting exercises to do at home, so your progress begins on day one.

What to bring

  • A photo ID
  • Your insurance card, or your claim information for auto MedPay or workers' comp
  • Your new patient forms, filled out
  • Your doctor's referral or prescription, if your plan needs one
  • A list of your medicines
  • Any imaging reports or surgery notes you have

What to wear

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in, and supportive shoes. We may need to see and move the area we're treating, so shorts help for a knee or hip, and a T-shirt or tank top helps for a shoulder or neck.

Book your whole plan before you leave

At the end of your evaluation, we schedule all your visits with you. That way your times are saved, and it's easier to keep going and finish strong. Need to change a visit later? Just call the clinic.
